This website is a static site. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics, and does
not track its visitors.

## Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P
Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the site, GitHub
receives technical data such as your IP address, which is required to deliver
the pages. Details are described in the [GitHub Privacy
Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

We do not have access to these server logs and store no personal data
ourselves.

## External content

Some pages embed videos from YouTube. The embedded player loads content from
YouTube (Google) servers when the page is opened; YouTube's [privacy
policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies to this.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to information about, correction of, and
deletion of personal data concerning you. Since we store no personal data,
such requests usually concern the hoster. For questions, contact the operator
named in the [imprint]({{< relref "/imprint" >}}).
